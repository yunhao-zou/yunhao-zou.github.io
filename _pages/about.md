I am a **Staff Algorithm Engineer at OPPO**, which I joined in July 2026. I received my **Ph.D.** in Computer Science and Technology from [Beijing Institute of Technology](https://bit.edu.cn/) in June 2025, advised by [Prof. Ying Fu](https://ying-fu.github.io/). From 2024 to 2025, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at [the University of Würzburg](https://www.uni-wuerzburg.de/), working with [Prof. Radu Timofte](https://www.informatik.uni-wuerzburg.de/computervision/). I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2019.

My research focuses on **computational photography** and **low-level vision**, especially image and video restoration and enhancement. My work combines **physics-based sensor noise modeling** with realistic data synthesis to reduce the sim-to-real gap. Building on this background, I am interested in **generative models for image and video generation**, including physically grounded generation and world models.

<p class="collaboration-note">I am enthusiastic about exploring how technology works and turning ideas into practical solutions. I welcome <strong class="text-emphasis">technical discussions and research collaborations</strong>. Feel free to connect!
</p>
